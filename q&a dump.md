1. Which of the following is the highest priority under the ISC2 Code of Ethics?
A. Protect the interests of principals
B. Protect society, the common good, and public trust
C. Advance and protect the profession
D. Act honestly and legally

2. A CISSP is asked to bypass company policy to speed up a project. Which canon of the ISC2 Code of Ethics is most directly at risk?
A. Provide diligent and competent service to principals
B. Protect society and the common good
C. Act honorably, honestly, and legally
D. Advance and protect the profession

3. An organizational code of ethics typically includes all of the following EXCEPT:
A. Confidentiality of sensitive information
B. Avoidance of conflicts of interest
C. Respectful workplace conduct
D. Global promotion of the cybersecurity profession

4. If an ISC2 Code of Ethics requirement conflicts with an organizational code of ethics, the CISSP should:
A. Always follow the organization’s rules, regardless
B. Follow whichever code is easier to comply with
C. Balance both and apply the stricter standard
D. Consult ISC2 for an exemption

5. Which example BEST reflects “providing diligent and competent service to principals”?
A. Refusing to work on any project that involves risk
B. Completing security tasks carefully and within policy
C. Focusing only on advancing your professional reputation
D. Ignoring minor compliance issues if deadlines are urgent

1. B
2. C
3. D
4. C
5. B
