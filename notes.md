### ❓ What are tangible and intangible assets?

**Known:** [ ] 
**Source:** ThorTeaches  

**Answer:**  
Tangible assets are physical and measurable (servers, laptops, buildings).  
Intangible assets are non-physical (data, brand reputation, intellectual property).

### ❓ What is a Content Delivery Network (CDN)?

**Known:** [ ]  
**Source:** ThorTeaches  

**Answer:**  
A distributed network of servers that serves content from the closest location to the user to reduce latency and improve availability.

### ❓ Sutherland, Biba, Clark-Wilson, Brewer-Nash, Goguen-Meseguer, Bell-LaPadula differences

**Known:** [ ]  
**Source:** ThorTeaches  

**Answer:**  
- **Bell-LaPadula:** confidentiality. No read up, no write down.  
- **Biba:** integrity. No write up, no read down.  
- **Clark-Wilson:** commercial integrity through well-formed transactions and separation of duties.  
- **Brewer-Nash (Chinese Wall):** prevents conflicts of interest based on prior access.  
- **Sutherland:** integrity through controlled state transitions and preventing improper information flow.  
- **Goguen-Meseguer:** non-interference. High actions must not influence low users.
